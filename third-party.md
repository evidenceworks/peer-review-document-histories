# Sources and rights

Original analysis code is covered by the MIT license. Original documentation and author-generated analytical fields are covered by CC BY 4.0. These licenses do not replace the rights of source authors, publishers or data services.

- **eLife:** article-family identifiers, source-document identifiers, roles, evaluation steps and publication dates derive from public eLife XML and DocMaps. The source-field corrections retained in `data/supporting_records.json` derive from eLife records. See https://elifesciences.org/ and the source documents for attribution and terms.
- **Europe PMC:** evaluation assertions and bibliographic relations derive from its public web services, https://europepmc.org/RestfulWebService.
- **Early Evidence Base:** intermediary representation and dossier pointers derive from public DocMaps, https://eeb.embo.org/.
- **Crossref:** declared preprint and review relations derive from registered metadata, https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/.
- **BMC Medicine:** attachment identifiers, roles and source URLs derive from publisher peer-review pages. Preservation was checked against Richardson, R. (2025), *BMC Medicine Peer Review Reports Downloaded 31 August 2025*, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17103639. The pre-existing archive and its report texts retain their original rights.
- **EMBO journals and Life Science Alliance:** dossier locations and case-specific disclosure classifications derive from public article-linked review-process files. The three source URLs and page references are retained in `data/disclosure_cases.csv`.

The repository distributes derived records, source locators and exact-text fingerprints. Fingerprints contain no review text; paired attachment digests record the historical BMC comparison. It does not distribute publisher PDFs, complete review texts, captured web pages or historical raw XML. Classification fields express this study's operational definitions and do not imply ownership of the underlying material.
